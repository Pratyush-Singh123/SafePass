## 🛠️ Step 1: Rapid Recording Setup

Choose any free tool:
1. **Loom (Easiest)**: Go to [loom.com](https://www.loom.com), click Record Screen + Mic (or Camera bubble optional).
2. **OBS Studio**: Set Display Capture + Mic, record at 1080p 30/60fps.
3. **Zoom / Google Meet**: Start an empty meeting with yourself, share screen with microphone on, and click Record to Computer.
